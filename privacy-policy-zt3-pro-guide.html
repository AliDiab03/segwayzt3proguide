Privacy Policy

ZT3 Pro Guide · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ZT3 Pro Guide Android app (the “App”) handles information. The App is a guide for electric scooter owners, with setup help, maintenance tips, accessories, videos and a PDF manual. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

In short: the App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos or contacts. The only data collected through the App is by Google AdMob, which we use to show ads.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there is no sign-up or login).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your photos, videos, files, contacts, microphone or camera. The App does not request these permissions.
  • Information about your scooter or any device you own. The App is a guide only and does not connect to your scooter.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

When you save a topic for later, the App keeps the list of saved topics in local storage on your device. This list is not sent to us. You can remove saved items inside the App, or clear all of this data by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

The App allows Android’s automatic backup. This means your saved topics may be included in your device’s backup to your own Google account, according to your device’s backup settings. We do not have access to that backup. You can turn it off in your device settings.

The in-app search runs on your device. What you type in it is not sent to us.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. The App may show banner ads, medium-rectangle ads inside lists, interstitial (full-screen) ads and app-open ads.

When ads are requested, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate region derived from it
  • Device and OS information (model, OS version, language, screen details)
  • App usage and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks)

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, to personalize ads where permitted, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive your personal data from Google. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your Ad Choices

You can control how ads are personalized from your device:

  • Android settings: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Opting out does not remove ads. It makes them less relevant to you.

5. Internet Access and External Links

The App uses the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions to load ads and web pages, and to detect whether you are online. Some content links to external websites, such as Google search results, YouTube search results, the Google Play page of the App, and our support, terms and privacy pages. These links open inside the App’s built-in browser. Those websites are run by third parties, such as Google and YouTube, and may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their own policies.

6. Data Security

The App uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) and does not allow unencrypted traffic. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we do not hold personal information that could be exposed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data about you, most such requests relate to data held by Google as an advertising provider, which you can manage through your Google account and device ad settings. You may contact us at any time with questions.
